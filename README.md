# OpenCTI datasets

[![Website](https://img.shields.io/badge/website-opencti.io-blue.svg)](https://www.opencti.io)
[![Slack Status](https://img.shields.io/badge/slack-3K%2B%20members-4A154B)](https://community.filigran.io)

The following repository is used to store the OpenCTI data samples used by the OpenCTI connectors for synchronize catalogs of sectors, regions and countries.

## 📊 Sectors and Subsectors

### 🌾 Agriculture and agribusiness
Private entities specialized in the growth, culture, transport and transformation of plants or livestock for food.

### 📦 Logistics
Entities managing the flow of goods, equipment, materials, supplies etc.

### 🎭 Culture and entertainment
Public and private entities dealing with cultural, sport activities and entertainment.
- **Culture** - Private and public entities involved in creating, producing, and distributing goods and services that are cultural in nature, or protecting it.
- **Sports** - Private and public entities involved in producing, organizing, promoting goods and services associated with sports.
- **Entertainment industry** - All entities involved in the conception and production of entertainment products, such as music, movies or video games.
- **Gambling** - All companies or public organizations involved in gambling activities, such as casino, money games, etc.

### 🎓 Education
Public or private entities operating to facilitate learning and acquiring knowledge and skills.
- **Universities** - Public and private institutions for higher education.
- **Schools** - Public and private institutions for all primary and secondary school levels.

### ⚡ Energy
Public and private entities operating to extract, store, transport and process fuel, entities managing energy plants and energy storage and distribution.
- **Electricity** - Public or private entities involved in the storage, transport and distribution of electricity to end customers.
- **Nuclear power (civilian use)** - Public and private entities involved in the extraction and transport of nuclear fuel, operating nuclear plants for civilian purposes.
- **Gas** - Public or private entities involved in exploration, extraction, refining, transporting and marketing of natural gas products.
- **Oil** - Public and private entities involved in the global processes of exploration, extraction, refining, transporting and marketing of petroleum products.
- **Renewable energies** - Public and private entities involved in the production of electricity based on renewable resources.

### 🚗 Transport
All entities involved in the movement of people or goods from one place to another.
- **Air transport** - All entities transporting people or goods by plane, managing or exploiting airports and structures, traffic authorities and plane manufacturers.
- **Rail transport** - Public and private entities managing rail infrastructures, rail transportation, exploiting intelligent rail transport systems and the rail industry.
- **Maritime transport** - All entities transporting people or good by naval means, managing or exploiting naval structures (ports), naval constructors and traffic authorities.
- **Road transport** - Public and private entities managing road infrastructures, road transportation, exploiting intelligent transport systems and the automotive industry.

### 🛡️ Defense
Public and private entities involved in the conception and production of weapons and the planning and conducting of military operations.
- **Defense ministries (including the military)** - Includes the military and all defense related-space activities.
- **Defense industry** - All private contractors involved in the manufacturing and sale of weapons and military or defense technologies.
- **Defense research and development** - R&D for defense or military purposes.

### 💰 Finance
Public and private entities involved in the allocation of assets and liabilities over space and time.
- **Banking institutions** - Credit institutions whose business consists in receiving repayable funds from the public and granting credit.
- **Financial organizations** - Asset managers and investment firms, that are neither banks nor insurance companies.
- **Insurance services** - Companies that provides insurance services to customers.
- **Market infrastructures** - Systems necessary for the smooth process of market financing operations.

### 🌐 Diplomacy
Public or private entities which are actors of or involved in international relations activities.
- **Ministries of foreign affairs** - Governmental entities in charge of the diplomatic action of the State.
- **Think tanks** - Research institutes and structures specialized in policy making and advocating on international relations subjects.
- **International organizations** - International organizations made up primarily of sovereign states.

### 🏛️ Government and administrations
Civilian government institutions and administrations of the executive and legislative branches.
- **Central administration and government** - All central administrations and government bodies in charge of the executive power.
- **Local administrations** - Public administrations and government bodies managing a local territory.
- **Legislative branch (parliamentary chambers)** - Legislative chambers of the state, in charge of voting the laws.

### 🏥 Health
Public and private entities involved in research, services and manufacturing activities related to public health.
- **Healthcare services** - Hospitals and other direct medical practice activities.
- **Pharmacy and drugs manufacturing** - Public and private entities involved in producing and selling medicinal products and drugs.
- **Healthcare research** - Entities involved in the scientific investigation of understanding health factors.

### 🏨 Hospitality
Private entities offering to customers' leisure activities and experiences.
- **Lodging industry** - Entities offering short-term dwellings in exchange for money.
- **Food and drinks businesses** - Businesses preparing and serving food and drinks to customers.
- **Travel and tourism industry** - Businesses operating in the field of travel for pleasure or business.

### 💻 Technologies
Private entities related to research, development, manufacturing and distribution of electronics, softwares, computers and IT products.
- **High-tech** - Businesses involved in the conception and production of high technological equipment.

### ❓ Unknown

### ⚖️ Judicial power (justice)
All judicial activities of the state and related private entities.

### 🏭 Manufacturing
Private entities transforming and selling goods, products and equipment.
- **Heavy industries** - Private entities working to transform raw materials into manufactured products.
- **Construction** - Private entities engaged in preparation of land and construction.
- **Retail (distribution)** - Distribution and sale of goods directly to the consumer.

### 📡 Telecommunications
Private and public entities involved in the production, transport and dissemination of information and communication signals.
- **Digital infrastructures** - All operators and entities in charge of operating and managing the digital infrastructures.
- **Hosting service providers**
- **Internet Service providers**
- **Medias and audiovisual** - Communication outlets used to deliver information.

### 💼 Consulting
Private entities providing expert advice in a specific field to external entities.
- **Legal consulting**
- **Information Technologies Consulting** - Includes Managed Services Providers (MSPs).
- **Engineering consulting**

### ⛏️ Mining
Entities in charge of extracting minerals and other geological materials from the Earth.

### 🔬 Research
Private and public entities such as university research centers, labs, experimental centers etc.

### 💧 Water distribution and supply
All public and private entities involved in the collection, transport and distribution of water for consumption purposes.

### 👥 Civil society
The general public and all non-governmental entities, or individuals independent of governments.
- **Non-Governmental Organizations (NGOs)** - A legally constituted non-commercial organization.
- **Citizens** - The general population of a country.
- **Political parties** - A recognized or non recognized political group or party.
- **Dissidents** - Individuals publicly disagreeing with and criticizing their government.
